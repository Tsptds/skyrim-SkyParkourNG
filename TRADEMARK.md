### Rebranding Scope
When redistributing a modified build, complete rebranding is required at all levels. 
The fork must change:
1. The project name on any hosting platform (e.g., Nexus Mods page title).
2. The distribution archive filename (.zip, .7z, .rar).
3. The internal compiled binary name (.dll) and its file properties/metadata (Product Name, Description).
4. Remove all official logos, header images, and brand identifiers.
